Invoicing guide · Slovenia

What must an invoice contain in Slovenia?

A Slovenian sole proprietor’s invoice needs identifying details and sale information; the exact requirements depend on VAT identification. Where fiscal confirmation is required, card payments count as cash payments too — Billero does not perform fiscal confirmation.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen · Pravilnik ZDDV-1, 141. člen · ZDavPR, 2. in 3. člen

Checked: November 2026 · Billero 1.8.1

For ordinary domestic supplies by a Slovenian sole proprietor. Special schemes, simplified invoices, cross-border transactions and public-sector invoices need separate checks.

Use Billero for direct bank transfers

Use Billero for invoices paid directly into a transaction account: for consumers and foreign customers, and for Slovenian businesses until 31 December 2027. From 1 January 2028, Slovenian businesses, sole proprietors included, must exchange domestic invoices with each other only as structured e-invoices (ZIERDED), so a Billero PDF will no longer be enough for them. Cash or card invoices that require fiscal confirmation need a suitable separate solution.

Source: GOV.SI — Davčno potrjevanje računov · ZIERDED (Uradni list RS, št. 85/2025) — e-računi od 1. 1. 2028

Which details belong on the invoice?

If you are identified for VAT

For a standard invoice under Article 82 of ZDDV-1, check:

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen
  • Issue date and a sequential number identifying the invoice.
  • Supplier and customer names and addresses; the supplier’s VAT identification number.
  • The customer’s VAT ID where the customer owes VAT or receives the relevant intra-EU supply under Article 46.
  • Quantity and type of goods, or extent and type of services.
  • Supply, completion or relevant advance-payment date, if known and different from the issue date.
  • Taxable amount by rate or exemption, unit prices excluding VAT and discounts not included in those prices.
  • VAT rate and amount, or the applicable exemption reference. Special cases need further wording, such as reverse charge.
Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen

If you are not identified for VAT

  • Issue date and sequential invoice number.
  • Supplier’s business/name and registered office or permanent address.
  • Selling price and total value excluding VAT.
  • For an invoice to another taxable person, the quantity and type of goods or extent and type of services.
Source: Pravilnik ZDDV-1, 141. člen

This is the minimum under Article 141 of the implementing rules. Customer details, a clear work description, service date, payment deadline and bank details are useful additions, rather than one identical statutory checklist for every supplier.

Source: Pravilnik ZDDV-1, 141. člen

Tax and registration numbers

Beyond the VAT rules, Article 35 of ZDavP-2 requires the issuer’s tax number on accounting documents, including when the issuer is not identified for VAT.

Source: ZDavP-2, 35. člen — davčna številka

ZGD-1 requires a sole proprietor’s business name, registered office and registration number on communications to an individual recipient, with an exception for the registration number in existing business relationships. Check these details for your case too.

Source: ZGD-1, 72. člen — podatki podjetnika

VAT exemption and thresholds

For a domestic small business, the basic threshold is €60,000 of annual Slovenian turnover, considering both the previous and current calendar year. If current-year turnover exceeds €60,000 but not €66,000, the exemption can continue until year-end, with identification from the next year. Exceeding €66,000 requires identification during the month of crossing.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen · FURS — Identifikacija za DDV

Being below the threshold does not rule out VAT identification: cross-border services or acquisitions can trigger separate requirements, and voluntary registration also matters.

Source: FURS — Identifikacija za DDV

If Article 94(1) actually applies, you can explain the exemption with this Slovenian wording:

DDV ni obračunan na podlagi 1. odstavka 94. člena ZDDV-1.

Billero uses this clause for a Slovenian issuer without charged VAT, including on an English PDF. Switching VAT off does not establish eligibility. Article 141 does not list this clause as a separate minimum field.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen · Pravilnik ZDDV-1, 141. člen

Cash, cards and UPN QR

Where fiscal confirmation is required, cash includes debit and credit cards. Direct transfers into a transaction account are excluded from that definition. Do not assume every online payment or intermediary payment is a direct bank transfer.

Source: ZDavPR, 2. in 3. člen

Billero’s UPN QR supplies bank-transfer details. It does not replace invoice information or fiscal confirmation; the actual payment method matters.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen · ZDavPR, 2. in 3. člen

How to do it in Billero

  • Select Slovenia in your business profile and check the address, tax details and VAT setting.
  • Choose the customer, add descriptions and line items, and check dates and invoice number.
  • Enter your IBAN for direct transfers and enable UPN QR if you want it on the PDF.
  • Review totals and any exemption clause, then share the PDF from your phone.

What Billero does not do

Billero 1.8.1 creates PDFs. It does not fiscally confirm cash invoices with FURS, file tax returns or issue structured e-invoices, which are mandatory for invoices between Slovenian businesses from 1 January 2028 (ZIERDED). It does not determine exemption eligibility or every transaction-specific requirement. A Billero PDF alone is insufficient for an invoice requiring fiscal confirmation.

Source: ZDavPR, 2. in 3. člen · ZIERDED (Uradni list RS, št. 85/2025) — e-računi od 1. 1. 2028

Frequently asked questions

Are invoice requirements identical with and without VAT?

No. Article 82 of ZDDV-1 covers standard invoices for VAT-identified suppliers; Article 141 of the implementing rules sets the minimum for suppliers without VAT identification. Beyond the VAT rules, ZDavP-2 also requires the issuer’s tax number.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen · Pravilnik ZDDV-1, 141. člen · ZDavP-2, 35. člen — davčna številka
Is the Slovenian exemption threshold still €50,000?

No. The basic annual threshold is €60,000. The €66,000 limit only allows the exemption to continue during the current year under Article 94; it does not replace the basic threshold.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen · FURS — Identifikacija za DDV
Can Billero issue a fiscally confirmed invoice?

No. Billero does not perform fiscal confirmation. It can prepare a PDF for direct bank transfers (to Slovenian businesses until 31 December 2027, after that only to consumers and foreign customers); for cash and card payments, check the fiscal-confirmation obligation and use a suitable solution.

Source: ZDavPR, 2. in 3. člen · ZIERDED (Uradni list RS, št. 85/2025) — e-računi od 1. 1. 2028
Does UPN QR confirm the invoice with FURS?

No. UPN QR helps fill in bank-transfer details. It does not send the invoice to FURS or replace fiscal confirmation.

Source: ZDavPR, 2. in 3. člen
Which exemption clause does Billero use for Slovenia?

It refers to Article 94(1) of ZDDV-1. Check that this specific exemption applies to your business and transaction before issuing the invoice.

Source: ZDDV-1, 82. in 94. člen
