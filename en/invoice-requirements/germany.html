Invoicing guide · Germany

What must an invoice contain in Germany?

German Kleinunternehmer using § 19 UStG have a specific minimum invoice checklist under § 34a UStDV; standard invoices follow § 14 UStG. Billero creates ordinary PDFs, not structured E-Rechnungen, so check the permitted format as well as the invoice details.

Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern · § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen

Checked: November 2026 · Billero 1.8.1

For ordinary domestic supplies by a business established in Germany. Small-value invoices, special schemes, cross-border transactions and public-sector invoices need separate checks.

Kleinunternehmer: issuing and receiving are different

The small-business exception covers issuing E-Rechnungen. Kleinunternehmer still need to be able to receive them from 2025; Billero does not provide that process.

Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern · BMF — E-Rechnung FAQ (März 2026)

Required invoice details

Kleinunternehmer: the § 34a UStDV minimum

  • Full names and addresses of supplier and customer.
  • Supplier’s tax number, VAT identification number or small-business identification number.
  • Issue date.
  • Quantity and type of goods, or extent and type of services.
  • Consideration as one total and a notice of the small-business exemption under § 19 UStG.
  • For self-billing by the customer: the designation “Gutschrift”.
Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern

A sequential invoice number and supply date are not listed in this special minimum. We still recommend them for traceability, together with payment terms and bank details.

Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern

Standard invoice charging VAT

  • Names and addresses of both parties and the supplier’s tax number or VAT ID.
  • Issue date and a unique sequential invoice number.
  • Quantity and type of goods or extent and type of services, plus the supply date.
  • Consideration broken down by tax rate, agreed reductions, and the VAT rate and amount.
Source: § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen

Exemptions and special cases require further or different wording, such as an exemption notice or “Gutschrift”. Check the specific transaction under §§ 14 and 14a UStG.

Source: § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen

§ 19 thresholds and the exemption notice

From 2025, the domestic small-business exemption requires previous-year turnover of no more than €25,000 and current-calendar-year turnover of no more than €100,000. In the year a business starts, the relevant limit is €25,000. These are turnover, not profit, limits; opting out of the scheme also matters.

Source: § 19 UStG — Kleinunternehmer · Bundesportal — Kleinunternehmer, Gründungsjahr

The transaction that crosses the current-year limit of €100,000, or €25,000 in the start-up year, is already outside this exemption. Earlier exempt transactions remain exempt.

Source: Bundesportal — Kleinunternehmer, Gründungsjahr

If you actually use the exemption, do not separately charge VAT and include a small-business exemption notice. One possible wording is:

Source: § 19 UStG — Kleinunternehmer · § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern

Gemäß § 19 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer berechnet.

The wording does not itself establish eligibility. Check your circumstances and the clause shown in the PDF before sending it.

Source: § 19 UStG — Kleinunternehmer

When is an ordinary PDF still allowed?

Since 2025, structured E-Rechnung is the default format for covered transactions between domestic businesses. An ordinary PDF is an “other invoice” (sonstige Rechnung). Sending such an electronic invoice requires the recipient’s consent.

Source: § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen
CaseOrdinary PDF
Kleinunternehmer under § 19The § 34a UStDV exception continues beyond the transition periods; PDF requires recipient consent.
Other issuers, 2025–2026 suppliesAllowed through December 31, 2026, with recipient consent.
Other issuers, 2027 suppliesAllowed through December 31, 2027 under this transition rule only if previous-year turnover is no more than €800,000; recipient consent required.
From 2028An ordinary PDF is insufficient for covered domestic B2B supplies unless an exception applies. The small-business exception continues.
Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern · § 27 Abs. 38 UStG — Übergangsregeln

This table concerns ordinary PDFs. Certain EDI procedures have a separate transition rule. Private consumers are outside the domestic B2B mandate; public-sector invoices have separate rules.

Source: § 27 Abs. 38 UStG — Übergangsregeln · BMF — E-Rechnung FAQ (März 2026)

Receiving from 2025: Kleinunternehmer must also be able to accept E-Rechnungen. BMF says an email inbox suffices for receipt; this alone does not discharge further processing and retention obligations.

Source: BMF — E-Rechnung FAQ (März 2026)

How to do it in Billero

  • Select Germany as your business country and check names, address and tax details.
  • Choose the customer and line items; check invoice number, supply date and payment deadline.
  • Set the VAT status to match your business and review either the exemption clause or charged VAT.
  • Create and share the PDF. Plus and Pro allow German or English as the document language.
  • For bank transfers, add bank details and an optional SEPA EPC payment QR.

What Billero does not do

Billero 1.8.1 creates ordinary PDFs, not XRechnung or ZUGFeRD with structured invoice data. It does not provide an E-Rechnung receipt or archiving workflow or file tax returns. If you must issue a structured e-invoice, you need another solution. Billero does not determine small-business eligibility or check recipient consent.

Frequently asked questions

Do Kleinunternehmer need a sequential invoice number?

The special minimum in § 34a UStDV does not list one, though it is useful for traceability. It is mandatory on standard invoices under § 14(4) UStG.

Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern · § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen
What are the Kleinunternehmer turnover limits?

From 2025, domestic businesses must have no more than €25,000 of previous-year turnover and no more than €100,000 in the current year. In the start-up year, the relevant limit is €25,000. Other conditions and any opt-out also matter.

Source: § 19 UStG — Kleinunternehmer · Bundesportal — Kleinunternehmer, Gründungsjahr
Must Kleinunternehmer issue E-Rechnungen?

For supplies exempt under § 19 UStG, § 34a UStDV still permits other invoices. An ordinary PDF needs recipient consent. The exception covers issuing, not the ability to receive e-invoices required since 2025.

Source: § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern · § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen · BMF — E-Rechnung FAQ (März 2026)
Is a PDF sent by email an E-Rechnung?

An ordinary PDF is not an E-Rechnung under the UStG definition applying since 2025 because it lacks structured data. Billero creates these ordinary PDFs.

Source: § 14 UStG — Ausstellung von Rechnungen
Can I use Billero during the transition period?

Ordinary PDFs are covered by the general transition through 2026 and, for 2027, the €800,000 previous-year turnover limit; recipient consent is needed. Kleinunternehmer have their own continuing exception. Check which rule applies to your transaction.

Source: § 27 Abs. 38 UStG — Übergangsregeln · § 34a UStDV — Rechnungen von Kleinunternehmern
