Billero – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (app version 1.8.1)

Billero is a local-first invoicing app. Your invoices, quotes, customers and business profile are stored on your device. There is no Billero account or login, we do not run servers that receive your business data, and we have no copy of it. This policy explains which limited data leaves your device, when, and to whom.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device, in a local database (SQLite) and in the app's files:

  • Issued and received invoices, quotes and related documents (PDFs, photos)
  • Customer information you enter (names, addresses, tax IDs, email addresses)
  • Your business profile (company details, tax numbers, bank account, payment link, crypto wallet addresses, logo, signature)
  • App settings and preferences

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device. We cannot restore it, so keep a backup (section 4).

2. When Data Leaves Your Device

2.1 Crash and error reports – Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC)

In the versions published in the stores, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. When the app crashes or records certain errors, Google receives:

  • The stack trace and the error message (it may contain technical details of the operation that failed)
  • Device model, operating system version and app version
  • A random installation identifier (Firebase Installation ID)
  • Short technical log lines about purchases (product and purchase state, never payment details)

We do not attach your name, email address or invoice content to these reports, and they are not used for advertising. Google keeps crash reports for a limited period under the Firebase terms. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.2 AI reading of received invoices – Anthropic PBC (USA)

AI reading is optional. Nothing is sent until you give consent in the app, and then only when you start reading a document. The selected or photographed document (image or PDF) is sent in full to Anthropic, which extracts the supplier or buyer, number, date, amount and description; the result is saved on your device. The document may contain personal and financial data. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train models; Anthropic may keep it for a limited period under its data retention policy. If you enter your own Anthropic API key, your own agreement with Anthropic applies. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app (Business tab). Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

2.3 Exchange rates for crypto payment QR codes – Frankfurter and CoinGecko

Only if you add crypto wallet addresses to your profile, the app fetches exchange rates when it builds an invoice PDF with crypto payment QR codes: from api.frankfurter.app (currency rates) and api.coingecko.com (crypto prices). The request contains only currency codes (for example USD or EUR), no invoice or customer data. As with any internet request, the provider sees your IP address.

2.4 Start-up message – billero.app

When the app starts, it may load a short message text from billero.app. No data about you or your invoices is sent; our web hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of the request).

2.5 Purchases – Apple App Store and Google Play

Subscriptions are bought and paid through Apple or Google, who handle your payment method and account. We do not receive your payment details. The app receives the confirmation of your purchase from the store on your device. We currently do not operate a server for checking purchase receipts; if we introduce one, we will update this policy before it is enabled.

2.6 Data you share yourself

When you send an invoice, export a CSV file or share a backup, the app uses your device's share sheet or email app. You choose the recipient or destination, and that service's terms apply. We do not receive a copy.

3. Permissions and Tracking

  • Camera and photos – to scan or select received invoices, your logo and signature
  • Biometrics or device PIN – for the optional app lock
  • Notifications – for reminders about due invoices; they are created on your device and never sent to us
  • Internet – for the services in section 2

The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking SDKs, does not use the advertising ID, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Backups

  • Automatic backup (Plus and Pro, when you turn it on): always encrypted with the password you set (AES-256, ZIP AES format). Without a password, no automatic backup is created.
  • Manual export (Business tab): the password is optional. With a password the ZIP file is encrypted with AES-256; without a password it is not encrypted, so store and share it carefully.
  • Export from the plans screen (before a purchase): the ZIP file is not encrypted.

File names inside a ZIP file are not encrypted, only their content. Your own API keys are included in a backup only if you switch this on (off by default). Backups are saved where you choose; we never receive them.

5. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and Similar Laws)

Your business data is on your device, so you can view, correct, export or delete it at any time in the app, or delete all of it by uninstalling the app. For crash reports (section 2.1) and other questions, write to info@billero.app; crash reports are linked only to a random installation identifier, not to your identity, so tell us what you need and we will help as far as the data can be identified. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

6. Children

Billero is a business tool for users aged 18 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. International Transfers

Google (Firebase Crashlytics) and Anthropic may process data in the United States or other countries. These providers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Security

Data on your device is protected by the security of your operating system (Android, iOS) and, if you turn it on, by the app lock. All connections described in section 2 use HTTPS.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.

10. Contact and Data Controller
