Privacy by design

Your invoices are not our business model.

Invoices, clients, images and settings are stored in a local database on your device. Billero has no advertising SDKs. OCR and crash diagnostics use external services described in the privacy policy. The App Store and Google Play process subscriptions.

Frequently asked questions

Before your first invoice.

How do I create an invoice on my phone?

Enter your business details, choose a client and add the service and amount. Billero creates a PDF you can share using your phone’s sharing options.

Do I need an account or registration?

No. You can start using the core app without creating a user account.

Where is my data stored?

Invoices, clients and settings are stored locally on your device. Optional OCR sends the selected document to an external provider; the privacy policy explains diagnostics and purchases.

How many documents are free?

In version 1.8.1, Free includes the first 10 invoices and quotes combined, up to 5 clients and 5 OCR scans per month. Converting a quote to an invoice does not use another slot; PDFs carry a Created with Billero watermark.

Does Billero send invoices to the tax authority?

No. Billero creates PDFs; it does not provide fiscalization or structured e-invoicing. You remain responsible for meeting your business’s obligations.

How do payment QR codes work?

Add a QR code for your own payment link; your client scans it and the chosen provider processes the payment. Bank-transfer QR options depend on the issuer’s country: UPN for Slovenia and SEPA EPC for supported European transfers.

Can I manage more than one company?

Yes. Pro keeps separate data for each company and lets you switch between them. For one company, start with Free or choose Plus for unlimited documents.
