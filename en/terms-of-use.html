2. Tiers and Pricing

Billero offers four tiers:

  • Free – €0. Includes your first 10 documents in total (invoices and quotes together; a quote converted into an invoice counts once; the limit does not reset), up to 5 clients, and 5 OCR scans per month. PDFs include a "Created with Billero" watermark. After the limit, you keep read access to your documents: you can open, share and export them and void invoices, but you cannot create new documents or edit existing ones.
  • Plus – Unlimited invoices, quotes and clients, no "Created with Billero" watermark, choice of PDF language (the language of your business's country or English, depending on the country), recurring invoices, 50 OCR scans per month, weekly encrypted auto-backup. Billing: monthly or yearly; the price in your currency is shown in the App Store or Google Play before you confirm the purchase.
  • Pro – All Plus features plus multiple businesses with an overview of all of them, and unlimited OCR scans. Billing: monthly or yearly; the price in your currency is shown in the App Store or Google Play before you confirm the purchase.
  • Business – Multi-user access and advanced integrations. Available in a future release.

Tier features and limits are described in the app's paywall and may be adjusted in future releases.

5. AI / OCR Features

AI reading of documents (OCR) is optional. When you use it to read a received invoice or to import an invoice you issued (a photo, or a PDF, JPG or PNG file), the whole document is sent to the AI provider Anthropic (USA), which returns the extracted data. A document is sent only after you have agreed in the app's consent dialog and started the reading. Monthly OCR limits are enforced per tier (5 Free, 50 Plus, unlimited Pro). We do not receive or keep the document; how Anthropic handles it is described in our Privacy Policy.
